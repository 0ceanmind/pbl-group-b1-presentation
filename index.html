SLO 4 · Effects on the circulation

Too thin. Too thick.

Normal

  • Viscosity ≈ 3× water

Anemia — too thin

  • Viscosity ↓ · cardiac output ↑ (3–4×)
  • Exertion → hypoxia, heart failure

Polycythemia vera — too thick

  • Viscosity ↑ (up to 10×) → sluggish flow
  • Cardiac output ≈ normal
